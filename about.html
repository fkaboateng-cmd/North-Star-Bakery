Made close to home

About North Star

We are a neighborhood bakery built around careful work, familiar flavors, and the belief that fresh baking brings people together.

Our story

A welcoming place for everyday moments

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: create honest baked goods and a warm place where commuters, families, neighbors, and event planners can find something worth sharing.

From a weekday loaf to a birthday cake, each order receives the same hands-on care.

What guides us

Our sourcing philosophy

Quality first

We choose dependable ingredients that support consistent flavor and texture.

Small batches

Thoughtful batch sizes help us focus on freshness and careful preparation.

Community-minded

We aim to work with nearby suppliers when practical and reduce unnecessary waste.
